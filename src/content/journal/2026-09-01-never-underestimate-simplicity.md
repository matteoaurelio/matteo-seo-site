Communicating clearly and straight to the point might be your best advantage in business.  

Allowing others understand your value proposition in less than a minute creates a competitive advantage hard to beat.  

Especially now. We are surrounded by filler, meaningless marketing words, making powerful and concise statements more important than ever.   

Products that can be explained and visualized in two or three words outperform those that need three sentences.   

The use of imagery, precise word choice and appropriate adjectives can evoke real feelings in your clients.   

Imagine that while you talk to them they can imagine the end result. The end state or a feeling that your business can help them manifest.  

That's the essence of success. Understanding the desired end state of your customer and bringing him or her to that place through your services and commitment.  

I had to learn that the hard way.   

I thought having the most complex solution was the best one.   

Then I realized, after taking on several commercial responsibilities across a portfolio of clients, that simplicity is the most powerful tool I have on my belt.   

Communicating clearly, directly and transparently might be your best advantage. Don't lose that in the flood of information.   

Hold on to simple, yet meaningful value propositions and your product will succeed.   

People will come. You'll build trust organically.
